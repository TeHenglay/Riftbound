# Riftbound – Gamepass & Monetization Specification

This document details the official, clean **Gamepass Catalog** for *Riftbound*.

All pay-to-win, immersion-breaking, and progression-skipping passes have been removed. The shop focuses strictly on **High Quality-of-Life (convenience)**, **time-saving multipliers**, and **prestigious VIP perks**.

## 1. 🧲 Infinite Magnet (Super Vacuum) — 199 Robux
* **Category**: Pure Quality-of-Life (Convenience)
* **The Problem**: In chaotic wave battles with 10+ enemies and anime bosses, running around the room picking up scattered gold and XP is tedious and risks taking avoidable damage.
* **Effect**: Automatically sucks all **Gold Coins and XP Orbs** directly into your character from anywhere in the arena with **infinite range**.
* **Why Players Buy It**: High-satisfaction staple purchase; allows 100% focus on combat and dodging.

---

## 2. 💰 2x Gold Booster — 249 Robux
* **Category**: Currency Progression Multiplier
* **Effect**: Permanently doubles all Gold earned from:
  * In-combat enemy kill drops.
  * Story Act Clear completion bounties (e.g. Act 3 Boss victory awards **5,000 Gold** instead of 2,500).
* **Why Players Buy It**: Accelerates saving up for Forge crafting fees (1,000 – 1,750 Gold), Skill Evolution ascensions (2,500 Gold), and permanent lobby upgrades.

---

## 3. 📈 2x XP Booster — 249 Robux
* **Category**: Leveling & Stat Points Multiplier
* **Effect**: Permanently doubles all XP gained from enemy kills ($+100\%$ XP rate).
* **Why Players Buy It**: Because Riftbound uses a steepened, controlled leveling curve ($100 \times \text{Level}^{1.65}$), players who want to level up faster and unlock attribute stat points quickly will buy this pass.

---

## 4. 📦 2x Match Drops (Double Clear Rewards) — 399 Robux
* **Category**: Material & Reward Multiplier
* **The Problem It Solves**: Farming enough Elemental Shards, Cores, and Rift Shards to evolve your base skills and forge fusions takes multiple runs.
* **Effect**: Permanently **doubles all item rewards earned from clearing a match/act**:
  * **2x Nation Shards** from Act clears (e.g. Cleared Act 1 awards **2–4 Shards** instead of 1–2).
  * **2x Nation Cores** from Act 2 clears (awards **2 Cores** instead of 1).
  * **2x Nation Essences** from Act 3 Boss victories (awards **2 Essences** instead of 1).
  * **2x Rift Shards** bundle from Act clears (awards **6 to 16 Rift Shards** instead of 3 to 8).
  * **Wave Clear Bonus**: Each wave cleared drops **2 Runes** instead of 1!
* **Why Players Buy It**: Halves the grinding time needed for Forge crafting and skill evolution.

---

## 5. 🍀 Lucky Runes (+25% Drop Luck) — 399 Robux
* **Category**: Loot & Build Enhancer
* **Effect**: Grants a permanent **+25% Luck Multiplier** to the post-wave Rune drop generator:
  * Significantly increases the odds of receiving **Epic (Purple)** and **Legendary (Gold)** Runes upon clearing waves.
* **Why Players Buy It**: Players hunting for build-defining runes (*Twin Cast*, *Cataclysm*, *Singularity*, *Endless Cascade*) will gladly buy luck to speed up finding their dream loadouts.

---

## 6. 👑 VIP: Rift Sovereign — 799 Robux
* **Category**: Premium Prestige & Ultimate Status
* **The Complete VIP Bundle**:
  1. **Visual Flex**: Glowing golden **`[VIP]` Chat Tag** and an exclusive radiant sovereign particle aura in Crossroads and lobbies.
  2. **Passive Boosts**: Permanent **+20% Gold and +20% XP boost** (stacks additively with the 2x boosters for a total of 2.2x!).
  3. **Daily Tribute**: Claim **10x Rift Shards + 2x Husk Ichor** for free every 24 hours from the Crossroads VIP altar.
  4. **VIP Lounge**: Access to the rooftop VIP Sky Lounge in the central Crossroads hub.

## ⚖️ Free-to-Play Integrity Check

* **Zero Paywalled Content**: Free players can earn every single Skill, Rune, Crafting Fusion, Evolution Ascension, and Anime Boss drop purely through gameplay and skill.
* **Flask Upgrades Remain Free**: Flask capacity (up to 5 charges) and potency (up to 40% heal) remain strictly purchasable with in-game Gold Coins in the Crossroads shop.
* **Starter Purity Preserved**: Every player starts with their chosen Nation's signature skill and unlocks other skills through Act 1 clears.
