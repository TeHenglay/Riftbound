# Riftbound — Skill Icons Design & Generation Guide

A comprehensive guide for generating and importing all 18 skill icons for **Riftbound** in the **Roblox Anime Battle Arena style** (clean 2D anime cel-shaded, zero borders, bold readability).

---

## 1. The Art Style Guide

### Target Aesthetic: Roblox Anime Battle Style
Inspired by *The Strongest Battlegrounds*, *Blox Fruits*, and *Anime Fighting Simulator*:
- **No 3D / No Borders:** No stone slabs, no runic rings, no beveled frames. The elemental effect fills the frame edge-to-edge.
- **Clean Graphic Silhouettes:** One unmistakable graphic shape per icon (e.g. sharp wind shuriken, curved dragon flame tongues, twisting tornado with central sheen, or blocky Roblox avatar in motion).
- **2-to-3 Tone Cel-Shading:** Crisp anime color bands with a **blinding white-hot core shine** instead of noisy photorealistic textures or gritty fantasy clutter.
- **Clean Dark Vignette Background:** Smooth dark navy / charcoal gradient background so the icon pops cleanly on small (48×48 px) player hotbars.

### Universal Negative Constraints
To prevent AI models from producing realistic clutter or noisy fantasy art, always include:
```text
No realistic textures, no 3D stone frames, no borders, no photo grain, no text, no watermark, no complex noisy background.
```

## 4. Free Generation Tools

If you want to generate them yourself right now for free:

1. **Microsoft Copilot / Bing Image Creator (Free DALL-E 3):**
   * URL: [bing.com/create](https://www.bing.com/create) or [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com)
   * Free, requires no credit card.
   * Paste any prompt from Section 3 above directly.

2. **Midjourney (Niji 6 Mode):**
   * Add `--niji 6 --no 3d, border, frame, text` to the end of any prompt.
