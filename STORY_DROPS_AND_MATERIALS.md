# Riftbound – Story Drops & Material Rewards Specification

This document details the reward distribution system for Story Mode across all 5 nations.

---

## 1. Core Rule: Zero Combat Clutter

* **During Combat (All Waves)**:
  * Enemies (Swarmers, Grunts, Brutes, Elites) **ONLY drop XP Orbs and Gold Coins**.
  * Enemies **NEVER drop crafting materials or items during combat**.
* **Reward Timing**:
  * **Wave Clear**: Grants **1 Rune** (for the 3-slot rune socketing system).
  * **Act Clear & Boss Victory**: Grants **Crafting & Evolution Materials** (Elemental Shards, Cores, Essences).

This keeps active combat clean, fast-paced, and focused on dodging and skill casting without players running around picking up item crystals.

---

## 2. The 3-Tier Elemental Material System

Each nation provides its own dedicated elemental materials used for **Skill Evolution** and **Forge Crafting**:

| Nation | Tier 1 (Act 1 Clear) | Tier 2 (Act 2 Clear) | Tier 3 (Act 3 Boss Clear) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🔥 Fire (Ember Dominion)** | **Fire Shard** | **Ember Core** | **Fire Essence** |
| **🌊 Water (Tidewater Court)**| **Water Shard** | **Abyssal Pearl** | **Water Essence** |
| **🪨 Earth (Stonehold)** | **Earth Shard** | **Granite Core** | **Earth Essence** |
| **🌪️ Wind (Skyreach)** | **Wind Shard** | **Zephyr Core** | **Wind Essence** |
| **⚡ Lightning (Stormcall)** | **Lightning Shard** | **Volt Core** | **Lightning Essence** |

*(Universal stabilizing materials: **Rift Shards** are awarded as bonus bundle rewards upon clearing acts).*

## 3. Act Clear Material Rewards & Drop Percentages

### 📍 Act 1: The Outskirts
*Focus: Tier 1 Shards for basic evolutions and base crafts.*

* **Guaranteed Materials**:
  * **1x Nation Shard** (100% Guaranteed)
  * **3x Rift Shards** (100% Guaranteed)
* **Chance-based Bonus Drops**:
  * **50% Chance**: **Nation's Signature Base Skill** (e.g. 50% for *Fireball* on Fire Act 1). Unlocks skill if not owned; gives +1 Level / Evolution copy if already owned!
  * **40% Chance**: `+1 Additional Nation Shard` (total of 2 Shards)
  * **10% Chance**: `+1 Husk Ichor`
* **Gold Bounty**: 500 Gold.

---

### 📍 Act 2: The Core / Forges
*Focus: Tier 2 Cores for advanced fusions and ascended skills.*

* **Guaranteed Materials**:
  * **1x Nation Core** (100% Guaranteed, e.g. Ember Core / Abyssal Pearl)
  * **1x Nation Shard** (100% Guaranteed)
  * **5x Rift Shards** (100% Guaranteed)
* **Chance-based Bonus Drops**:
  * **50% Chance**: `+1 Additional Nation Shard` (total of 2 Shards)
  * **25% Chance**: `+1 Additional Husk Ichor`
* **Gold Bounty**: 1,000 Gold.

---

### 📍 Act 3: Throne / Mountain Heart (Boss Victory)
*Focus: Tier 3 Essences for pinnacle ascension and legendary power.*

* **Guaranteed Materials**:
  * **1x Nation Essence** (100% Guaranteed, e.g. Fire Essence)
  * **1x Nation Core** (100% Guaranteed)
  * **2x Nation Shards** (100% Guaranteed)
  * **8x Rift Shards** (100% Guaranteed)
* **Chance-based Bonus Drops**:
  * **35% Chance**: `+1 Additional Nation Core`
  * **50% Chance**: `+1 Additional Nation Shard` (total of 3 Shards)
* **Boss Gold Bounty**: 2,500 Gold.
* **Special Bonus**: Guaranteed **1 Legendary Rune choice** (Pick 1 of 3).
* **Anime Boss Signature Skill Drops (Difficulty Separation)**:
  * **Normal Difficulty**: **100% Guaranteed 1st Skill Drop** (Iconic Offensive Nuke: *Rasenshuriken*, *Chidori*, *Fire Fist*, *Vagabond Drill*, *Bedrock Rupture*). Skill 2 never drops here.
  * **Hard Difficulty (Exclusive)**: **100% Guaranteed 2nd Skill Drop** (*Multi-Shadow Clone Jutsu*, *Kirin*, *Great Flame Emperor: Entei*, *Ocean Current Geyser*, *Granite Armor*). The 2nd skill is strictly exclusive to Hard Mode and cannot drop in Normal mode.
  * **Repeat Clears**: Subsequent clears offer a **30% chance** to drop duplicate copies to level up that difficulty's skill (up to Max Level 5).

## 4. Difficulty Multipliers for Act Clear Rewards

Playing story acts on higher difficulty levels scales up material payouts:

| Difficulty | Enemy HP / Damage | Material Multiplier | Act Clear Rewards |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Easy** | 1.0x / 1.0x | **1.0x** | Base rewards listed above. |
| **Medium** | 1.6x / 1.4x | **1.5x** | +50% Gold, +1 bonus Shard on Act 1/2. |
| **Hard** | 2.4x / 1.9x | **2.0x** | 2x Shards guaranteed, higher Core drop chances. |
| **Nightmare**| 3.6x / 2.6x | **3.0x** | 3x Shards, guaranteed 2x Cores on Act 2/3. |

## 5. Crafting & Awakening Cost Table (Balanced & Earned)

With materials earned exclusively through Act Clears, recipe costs are balanced so that **Skill Awakening is a major milestone that cannot be rushed early in Act 1**:

### A. Skill Awakening (Awakened Form)
* **Requirements**:
  1. Base Skill must be at **Max Level 5**.
  2. **1x Nation Essence** (Act 3 Boss clear) **OR 2x Nation Cores** (Act 2 clears).
  3. **8x Nation Shards** (requires multiple Act 1 / Act 2 runs).
  4. **15x Rift Shards**.
  5. **2,500 Gold**.
* **Examples**:
  * **Fireball Awakened**: Fireball (Lv 5) + 1x Fire Essence (or 2x Ember Core) + 8x Fire Shard + 15x Rift Shard + 2,500 Gold.
  * **Tidal Wave Awakened**: Tidal Wave (Lv 5) + 1x Water Essence (or 2x Abyssal Pearl) + 8x Water Shard + 15x Rift Shard + 2,500 Gold.
  * **Stone Spike Awakened**: Stone Spike (Lv 5) + 1x Earth Essence (or 2x Granite Core) + 8x Earth Shard + 15x Rift Shard + 2,500 Gold.
  * **Gust Awakened**: Gust (Lv 5) + 1x Wind Essence (or 2x Zephyr Core) + 8x Wind Shard + 15x Rift Shard + 2,500 Gold.
  * **Spark Bolt Awakened**: Spark Bolt (Lv 5) + 1x Lightning Essence (or 2x Volt Core) + 8x Lightning Shard + 15x Rift Shard + 2,500 Gold.

### B. Dual-Element Crafting at the Forge
* **Tier 1 Fusions (Utility & Control)**:
  * Base Skill A + Base Skill B + **4x Shard A** + **4x Shard B** + **8x Rift Shard** + **1,000 Gold**.
  * *Examples:* Steam Cloud, Mudslide, Frost Gale, Quake Leap.
* **Tier 2 Fusions (Heavy Hitters & Pinnacles)**:
  * Base Skill A + Base Skill B + **5x Shard A** + **5x Shard B** + **1x Core** + **12x Rift Shard** + **1,750 Gold**.
  * *Examples:* Magma Meteor, Plasma Lance, Magnet Quake, Thunderstorm.
